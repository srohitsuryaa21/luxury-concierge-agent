# Model Positioning

Phantom is the ceremonial flagship for clients who value presence, privacy, and a
chauffeur-led experience. It is strongest for formal events, legacy clients, and
highly bespoke commissions.

Ghost is the discreet driver's saloon. It is appropriate for entrepreneurs and
executives who want luxury without theatrical presence.

Cullinan is the all-terrain luxury SUV for families, estates, mountain routes,
regional travel, and clients who need commanding practicality.

Spectre is the electric grand tourer for clients who want a quiet, modern,
sustainable luxury experience with effortless power delivery.
