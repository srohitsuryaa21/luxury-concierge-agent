# Availability And Lead Times

Typical lead times are directional. Available allocations usually take 6-9
months. Limited allocations usually take 9-14 months. Waitlisted configurations
can take 14-18 months.

EU availability is strongest for Ghost, Cullinan, and Spectre. Phantom is limited
because flagship commissions require more bespoke planning.

GCC demand is high for Phantom, Ghost, and Cullinan. Spectre is limited but
strategically important for sustainability-led clients.
