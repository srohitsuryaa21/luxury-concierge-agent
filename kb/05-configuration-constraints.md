# Configuration Constraints

Highly formal chauffeur briefs should prioritize Phantom or Ghost, black or deep
blue exterior finishes, privacy features, rear theatre configuration, and calm
cabin materials.

Family and estate briefs should prioritize Cullinan, durable cabin materials,
all-terrain practicality, rear comfort, and strong luggage flexibility.

Sustainability-led briefs should prioritize Spectre, responsible material
provenance, regenerative drive education, and lighter modern cabin finishes.
