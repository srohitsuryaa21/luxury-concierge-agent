# Materials And Cabin Mood

Grace White leather creates a formal, bright, ceremonial cabin. Ardent Tan gives
warmth and craft character. Scivaro Grey technical textile with responsibly
sourced leather accents is the recommended sustainable cabin direction.

Open-pore Circassian walnut supports warm, heritage-led cabins. Piano Black
technical veneer supports modern, formal, and electric configurations.

The starlight headliner, illuminated fascia, bespoke audio tuning, and rear
theatre configuration are useful signature options when the brief emphasizes
occasion, comfort, or client delight.
